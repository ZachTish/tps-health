# TPS Health 0.10.1

## What changed

- Made Describe resumable across an app close with stable TPS AI Gateway durable job IDs and a device-local workflow checkpoint.
- On reopen, Health reuses completed extraction/review results, searches saved foods plus bounded USDA and Open Food Facts sources, runs its existing plausibility checks, creates the selected food or meal notes, and restores the tray for the original date context.
- Checkpointed prepared tray data and created meal paths before final handoff to prevent repeated AI work or duplicate meal creation after a restart.
- Made single-item Describe results create a local food note before entering the tray, consistent with multi-item meals.

## Compatibility and privacy

- Backward-compatible patch; no settings or note migration is required.
- Describe text prompts/schemas use TPS AI Gateway's existing synced queue and 48-hour retention for cross-device recovery. Nutrition Facts photos remain device-local and never enter that queue.
- Minimum supported Obsidian version: 1.12.0.

## Validation

- The complete Health suite ran 165 checks: 164 passed and the one credential-gated live USDA check was intentionally skipped. Coverage includes three Home, two Daily Note date-owner, three property-catalog, 140 provider/search/workout/food-log checks with 139 passed, ten Describe checks, and seven routed-settings checks.
- The mandatory separate production-mode build passed and reported the final test runtime unchanged. Obsidian 1.13.7 was reloaded in the isolated test vault; **TPS Health: Log food** opened with the four Scan/Search/Saved/Describe modes, and Describe showed its research/self-review tray guidance and primary **Build tray** action. No food was logged, no note or setting was changed, and the credential-free vault sent no provider request.
- Source and test-runtime release artifacts were byte-identical. Production plugin files were not inspected or deployed.
