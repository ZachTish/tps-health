# TPS Health 0.37.1

TPS Health 0.37.1 fixes severe underestimation of multi-item composed meals in Describe.

## Fixes

- Parses written fractions such as `a half of` before portion estimation.
- Keeps an egg-and-cheese bowl and a half egg-and-bacon bagel sandwich as two independent tray rows.
- Sizes bowls and sandwiches by their dish form instead of prematurely treating either as one loose egg.
- Audits whether estimates include conservative calories for explicitly named egg, cheese, bacon, and bagel components; underestimates are retried per item.
- Requires saved/provider fallback matches to cover the meaningful components of a composed dish, preventing a generic egg record from replacing the whole dish.
- Strengthens cloud prompts around fractions and component-by-component composed-dish totals, and advances durable stage IDs so pending requests cannot reuse the old response contract.

## Compatibility

- No settings or data migration.
- Existing food notes, completed logs, pending trays, provider configuration, and native-record schemas remain compatible.
- Minimum supported Obsidian version remains 1.12.0.

## Validation

- Exact reported phrase covered by parser, semantic-audit, and end-to-end AI workflow regressions.
- Provider suite, full declared suite, TypeScript, and separate production-mode build run against the final versioned source.
- Final artifacts deploy only to the isolated test-vault runtime; production is not accessed.
