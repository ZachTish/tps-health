# TPS Health 0.4.1

TPS Health 0.4.1 refines the mobile and narrow-pane food-log chip layout based on an iPhone Live Preview screenshot.

## User-visible change

- The food title now receives all remaining width on the first row, with calories aligned at the right and the action menu at the far end.
- Serving, converted amount when present, and the remaining macro pills now occupy the second row together.
- Macro pills no longer squeeze ordinary food names into a tiny truncated column.
- Calories are identified by their `kcal` label instead of assumed to be the first nutrition value, preserving legacy and reconstructed records.

## Compatibility and migration

- Minimum supported Obsidian version: 1.12.0.
- No settings, note-data, parser, total, or API migration is required.

## Validation

- The declared suite passed 129 runnable checks; the one credential-gated live USDA request was skipped.
- Focused behavior tests cover calories first, calories in a later position, and macro lists without calories. Source/CSS regressions verify the explicit title/calorie and details rows.
- The mandatory standalone build deployed only to the isolated test vault. Obsidian 1.13.7 was reloaded, and the archived long-name fixture visually confirmed the requested hierarchy without editing a note, logging food, or calling a provider.
- Production was not accessed.
