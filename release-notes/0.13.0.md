# TPS Health 0.13.0

TPS Health 0.13.0 makes natural-language meal estimates fast and useful through TPS AI Gateway, and gives missing grocery barcodes a stronger automatic recovery path.

## User-visible changes

- **Describe** now uses one structured Gemini request to turn a typed or dictated meal into an editable tray. It preserves explicit quantities, units, brands, preparation, and separate sides/drinks while estimating nutrition for the whole described amount.
- Described items are one-off estimates. They can be edited, removed, replaced, or combined with searched foods, but they do not create reusable food or meal notes. Nothing is logged until **Log selected** succeeds.
- A missing barcode automatically tries Google-grounded Gemini research. Complete coherent evidence opens an editable food review; identity-only or uncertain evidence pre-fills the Nutrition Facts scanner; provider failure still leaves photo and manual entry available.
- The reviewed built-in grocery fallback now resolves Michelob Ultra Organic Seltzer Signature Collection from UPC `018200202636` (and equivalent EAN forms) or common name aliases as one 355 mL can: 80 kcal, 0 g protein/carbohydrate/fat, 11.2 g estimated alcohol, and 5 mg sodium.

## Evidence and safety

- Michelob's product identity and UPC are supported by [Target's current listing](https://www.target.com/p/-/A-81805144); [Food Lion](https://foodlion.com/groceries/product/michelob-ultra-seltzer-signature-collection-organic-12-pk-12-oz-cans/302244), [H-E-B](https://www.heb.com/product-detail/michelob-ultra-organic-seltzer-variety-6-pk-cans/5002565), and [Anheuser-Busch's launch information](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/michelob-ultra-introduces-first-national-usda-certified-organic-hard-seltzer-thats-as-real-as-it-tastes-with-the-launch-of-michelob-ultra-organic-seltzer-301207556.html) agree on a 12 fl oz can, 80 calories, 4% ABV, and zero carbohydrate. Alcohol grams are derived from 4% ABV, 355 mL, and standard ethanol density so energy remains physically coherent.
- Grounded product results are accepted only when serving and nutrition evidence is complete and calories are plausible against protein, carbohydrate, fat, sugar alcohol, and alcohol. Otherwise TPS Health carries identity into label capture and does not invent a reusable record.
- TPS AI Gateway 0.4.0 or newer with Gemini configured on the requesting device is required for AI Describe and grounded barcode recovery. The deterministic local Describe fallback, Nutrition Facts photo capture, and manual entry remain available as appropriate.

## Compatibility and migration
