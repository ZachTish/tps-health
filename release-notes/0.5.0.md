# TPS Health 0.5.0

TPS Health 0.5.0 is a backward-compatible grocery-search feature release. It makes saved foods easier to name the way you naturally search, tolerates reordered words and small typing errors, and retains more useful product identity data from USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts.

## User-visible changes

- Food, recipe, and meal editors now include **Search aliases** for comma-separated nicknames, store labels, and former package names. Aliases remain normal Markdown frontmatter and can be added, changed, or fully cleared.
- Local and provider-result matching is word-order independent and supports adjacent-character transpositions such as `peantu`/`peanut`. Long known-brand typos such as `kirklnad` are canonicalized conservatively without turning ordinary words such as `queso` into a brand.
- USDA results prefer the consumer brand, retain a different corporate owner plus alternate descriptions as aliases, and preserve GTIN/UPC and ingredients. Open Food Facts results retain alternate, generic, abbreviated, store, brand-tag, and category identities while keeping ingredients as lower-weight context.
- Provider result windows increase to 50 USDA rows with Branded enabled (25 otherwise) and 40 Open Food Facts rows. USDA uses one strict request plus at most one relaxed retry after a usable-result miss; Open Food Facts uses one primary route plus at most one legacy fallback.
- Equivalent 11-, 12-, and 13-digit provider barcodes now share local lookup, upsert, provider-cache, and result-deduplication identities. Duplicate USDA GTIN revisions use the newest published record and are omitted when that newest duplicate lacks a complete protein/carbohydrate/fat field rather than silently borrowing older nutrition.
- Provider backoff honors `Retry-After`, avoids fallback bursts after HTTP 429, and continues serving valid cached searches while the circuit is open. Open Food Facts liquid serving quantities retain milliliters rather than being treated as grams.

## Compatibility and migration

- Minimum supported Obsidian version remains 1.12.0.
- No settings, note-data, API, or manual migration is required. Existing `aliases` frontmatter remains compatible.

## Validation

- The final declared suite passed 133 automated checks; the one credential-gated live USDA integration check was skipped.
- Focused coverage includes alias create/preserve/clear behavior, comma-name inferred-alias safeguards, word reordering, transpositions, safe provider-brand canonicalization, strict-to-relaxed USDA retry, Open Food Facts/USDA cache behavior during backoff, provider serving units, newest-revision nutrition safeguards, and 11-/12-/13-digit barcode equivalence.
- The production-mode artifact was deployed only to the isolated test vault. Obsidian 1.13.7 was reloaded with **Reload app without saving**; local Search and its explicit online action were inspected, and an unsaved custom-food editor visibly exposed **Search aliases** and its comma-separated guidance.
- No food was created or logged, no online search was triggered during UI QA, and the prior pending search/tab values were restored. Opening the logger refreshed only its expected transient draft timestamp. Production was not accessed.

## Known limitations

- Search-as-you-type remains local-only; press Enter or choose **Search online** to query provider databases.
- Grocery coverage still depends on the provider databases and their nutrition completeness. Aliases improve discovery of saved or returned products but cannot manufacture a product that neither provider contains.
- Physical iPhone behavior should receive the normal on-device acceptance check after the BRAT pull.
