# TPS Health 6.0.2 — Apply tracked nutrients to food definitions

Reusable food, recipe and meal writers previously bypassed the tracked-nutrient selection applied to logs in 6.0.1. Creation, custom food templates and explicit definition edits now use **Food & goals → Your nutrient goals**, including **Track only**. Unselected nutrients are omitted before the existing writer; selected zeroes and custom nutrients remain supported. A polyol conversion factor is retained with selected sugar alcohol. No new settings schema or transport is introduced.

Filtering happens after nutrition calculation. Recipe ingredients, serving information, IDs, classification and authored body text remain intact. Existing untracked fields are not swept, deleted or overwritten by untracked incoming values. Changing settings does not rewrite notes. New definitions cannot recover an omitted nutrient later without another lookup or manual entry. This replaces 6.0.1's complete-definition policy. The app retains its independent Food properties selection and needs no runtime patch for this behavior.

Backward-compatible correctness patch; minimum Obsidian **1.12.0**. No new scan, watcher, poller, cache or recovery owner. Downgrading restores all-nutrient definition writes but cannot recover data never saved. Physical iPhone acceptance is not claimed. Production is unchanged; this release is a BRAT handoff after test-vault validation.
