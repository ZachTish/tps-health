# TPS Health 0.4.3

This patch hardens the 0.4.2 responsive food-log layout at extremely narrow editor widths.

## Changes

- Gives the food title and calories explicit, non-overlapping first-line regions.
- Ellipsizes long titles before the reserved calorie label instead of allowing text to paint underneath it.
- Preserves the compact serving/macro/menu line, source Markdown, nutrition totals, settings, stored data, and plugin APIs.

## Validation

- 129 runnable automated checks passed; the credential-gated live USDA test was skipped.
- The production-mode artifact was deployed and hot-reloaded in the isolated test vault on Obsidian 1.13.7.
- The restored three-pane layout visually verified short and deliberately long titles at roughly 290 px, with right-aligned calories and the compact second line intact.
- Minimum supported Obsidian version remains 1.12.0.

## Known limitations

- Physical iPhone appearance should receive the normal on-device acceptance check after the BRAT pull.
