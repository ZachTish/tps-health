# TPS Health 0.3.7

TPS Health `0.3.7` is a backward-compatible Saved Foods performance release. It removes two redundant ranking passes while preserving the complete released Health feature set.

## Changes

- `FoodSearchModal.renderQuickPicks()` now consumes the ranked, deduplicated order already returned by `getSavedFoods()`.
- It collects the first eight foods with logged usage, records their selection keys once, and then collects the first eight remaining foods.
- Recent and frequent foods, My foods, headings, order, exact object identities, usage semantics, selection keys, empty state, and result actions are unchanged.
- Food providers, local search, online search, barcode and camera flows, serving math, logging, settings, stored data, commands, public APIs, compatibility routes, and fallbacks are unchanged.

## Before/after evidence

- Exact public `0.3.6`, local `main`, and `origin/main` were pinned to commit `00553640c0b80f87faca6436a935eafe8b1b9dba`.
- The released and candidate actual modal classes produced zero output, order, or identity mismatches across 1,000 randomized scenarios containing 120,784 foods.
- An independent actual-class adversary added 3,000 randomized scenarios containing 97,059 foods, including ties, colliding selection keys, and both stale-render routes, with zero heading, order, identity, or action mismatches.
- With 1,000 already ranked foods, the released quick-pick path performed 2,000 additional serving-metric reads; `0.3.7` performs zero.
- The complete Saved pipeline falls from three ranking/scoring passes to one.
- Across 31 alternating 12,000-food rounds, median time fell from 240.168 ms to 82.530 ms (65.64%) and p95 from 249.757 ms to 88.275 ms (64.66%).
- The independent 1,000-food benchmark measured 15.008 ms to 4.573 ms (69.53%), consistent with the larger benchmark.
- The production bundle changes from 901,718 to 901,775 bytes, exactly +57 bytes.

## Validation

- A focused actual-modal regression pins a nontrivial usage reorder, headings, exact object identities, recent/local ordering, and the no-rescore contract.
- The Health provider suite passes 106 of 107 checks, with only the credential-gated live USDA test skipped.
- The full declared suite passes 127 of 128 checks with only that credential-gated test skipped; TypeScript validation and the mandatory separate production-mode build pass.
- The versioned artifact deployed only byte-changed `main.js` and `manifest.json` to the isolated test runtime. Obsidian 1.12.7 reloaded with all nine Health commands registered, and the palette was dismissed without opening a modal, logging food, changing settings, or calling a provider.
- Source and test-runtime artifacts are byte-identical. Runtime `data.json` remains SHA-256 `17bd273fc88c814d0d17336acafe9f813de58e6e4a897394ad6849c57f0a0fc0`, and `.hotreload` remains unchanged.
- Production is not accessed or modified.

## Compatibility and limitations

- Minimum supported Obsidian version remains `1.12.0`.
- No settings or data migration is required.
- The optimization relies on the existing internal contract that `getSavedFoods()` returns its catalog ranked and deduplicated; the new regression pins that contract.
- AI/local parsing, Open Food Facts compatibility, credential rotation, barcode routes, GCM/native standalone writes, mobile scanning, and workout editor synchronization remain because they preserve released behavior or prevent data loss.
