# TPS Health 0.19.1

This backward-compatible patch makes branded, multi-word grocery searches reliable when a provider stores the brand in the product name instead of its dedicated brand field.

## Food search

- Open Food Facts receives one typo- and order-normalized brand-first phrase without a hard `brands:` restriction. This retains products whose community metadata has a blank or incomplete brand field.
- A provider row whose product name is only a recognized brand can combine that identity with its generic name, producing a useful result such as `White Claw Surge Hard Seltzer` rather than only `White Claw`.
- Reordered brand/product queries continue to share the same bounded provider cache entry.
- White Claw Surge includes a reviewed US 12 oz fallback for transient/incomplete provider results: 160 kcal, 2 g carbohydrate, 2 g sugar, approximately 22.4 g alcohol at 8% ABV, and 30 mg sodium. Flavor and package-specific provider records remain available when present. White Claw's official support confirms Surge at 8% ABV: https://ussupport.whiteclaw.com/en/support/solutions/articles/36000314438-how-much-alcohol-is-in-white-claw-hard-seltzer-surge-

## Compatibility

- No settings, note-storage, native-record, or public-API migration is required.
- Existing saved-food ranking, typo tolerance, provider rate limits, serving enrichment, and duplicate protection remain in place.
- Minimum Obsidian: 1.12.0. GCM 1.38.0 or newer remains required only for native-record mode.
