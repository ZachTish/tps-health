# TPS Health 0.14.2

TPS Health 0.14.2 prevents Describe from turning an unavailable match into an apparently complete zero-nutrition food.

## User-visible fixes

- An all-zero Gemini response is now treated as incomplete and retried instead of being accepted as a finished estimate.
- Clearly identified zero-nutrition items, including plain water, remain valid when Gemini is confident that the zeros are intentional.
- If Gemini review and repair are still unusable and no saved or curated food matches, Health makes one dedicated final Gemini estimate request based on the food's identity, amount, preparation, size, and estimated weight.
- The final pass asks Gemini for its best reasonable total nutrition using the closest ordinary analogue. A physically coherent low-confidence result stays editable in the tray rather than becoming an empty food.
- Describe estimates remain one-off log items and do not create reusable food notes.

## Compatibility and migration

- No settings migration.
- No note-schema migration.
- No public API change.
- TPS AI Gateway with Gemini configured is required for AI Describe.
- Minimum supported Obsidian version remains 1.12.0.

## Validation

- Final declared suite: 180 of 181 checks passed; the only skip was the credential-gated live USDA check.
- Focused coverage forces review and repair to return all-zero nutrition, forces saved/curated matching to miss, then verifies the dedicated Gemini estimate produces and retains a coherent 240 kcal estimate with protein, carbohydrate, and fat.
- Unit coverage verifies unexplained zeros produce a `nutrition-empty` audit issue while a confident plain-water result is accepted.
- The final artifacts deployed only to the isolated test runtime. Production was not accessed.
